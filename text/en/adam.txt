Adam (peace be upon him) was the first human being and the first Prophet. Allah created him from clay and breathed life into him, then taught him the names of all things - a knowledge the angels themselves did not have. When Allah commanded the angels to prostrate before Adam in honor, all of them obeyed except Iblis, who refused out of pride and was cast out, becoming the sworn enemy of mankind.

Allah created Hawwa (Eve) as Adam's companion, and the two lived in Paradise, free to enjoy everything except the fruit of one particular tree. Iblis, determined to mislead them, whispered doubts until they ate from it - a moment of forgetfulness and weakness, not defiance. Realizing their error, Adam and Hawwa turned to Allah in sincere repentance, and He forgave them.

They were sent down to the earth, which was always the intended place for mankind - not as a punishment without hope, but as the beginning of a trial. Adam became the first Prophet, teaching his children to worship Allah alone, to be truthful, and to be just with one another - laying the foundation for every generation of humanity that followed.
