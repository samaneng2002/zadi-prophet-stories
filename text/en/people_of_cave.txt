A group of young men lived in a town ruled by a tyrant who forced everyone to worship idols. Believing firmly in the One true God despite the danger, they fled together to a cave, praying: "Our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and prepare for us right guidance in our affair."

Allah caused them to fall into a deep sleep that lasted, by the Quran's own account, three hundred years and nine more - turning them from side to side so their bodies would not decay, while their dog stretched its paws at the cave's entrance, standing guard through it all. When they eventually woke, they had no sense of how long it had been, guessing only a day or part of a day had passed.

One of them went into the town to buy food, only to discover that generations had passed, the tyrant king was long gone, and belief in Allah was now openly practiced. Their story became a sign to the people of that later age, proving the resurrection and reminding everyone that Allah's promise of an afterlife is true, however long the wait may seem.
