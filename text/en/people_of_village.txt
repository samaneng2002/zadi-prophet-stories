Allah gives the example of a town to which He sent two messengers, but the townspeople rejected them and called them liars. He then strengthened them with a third messenger, and the three of them together said plainly: "Indeed, we are messengers to you." The people replied that these were only men like themselves, and accused them of lying, threatening to stone them if they did not stop their preaching.

At that moment, a man came running from the farthest part of the city, urging his people: "O my people, follow the messengers, follow those who ask no payment of you, and who are rightly guided." He reminded them there was no reason he should not worship the One who created him, to whom they would all be returned - and he openly declared his own belief for everyone to hear, even at personal risk.

His own people killed him for saying it, but Allah honored him immediately, telling him to "enter Paradise," at which point he wished his people could know how his Lord had forgiven him and made him among the honored. Allah then sent a single blast upon the town and its people were silenced at once, closing the story with a firm reminder that mocking Allah's messengers never goes unanswered, however delayed the reckoning may seem.
