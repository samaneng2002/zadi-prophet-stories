Idris (Enoch, peace be upon him) is mentioned in the Quran as a man of truth and a Prophet, raised by Allah to a high station. Islamic tradition remembers him as one of the earliest people given wisdom and knowledge - some accounts describe him as the first to write with a pen, to study the movements of the stars, and to work with tailoring and measurement.

He lived a life devoted to worship, patience, and gratitude to Allah, encouraging his people to righteousness at a time when few were willing to listen. The Quran tells us only briefly of him, describing him as truthful and patient, and that Allah raised him to a lofty place - a mark of honor for a Prophet whose detailed story was not given to us in full, unlike some others.

Idris's account is a reminder that a person's true worth before Allah is not measured by how much of their story is recorded for later generations, but by the sincerity of their devotion during their own lifetime.
