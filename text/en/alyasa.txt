Al-Yasa (Elisha, peace be upon him) is mentioned in the Quran alongside other righteous Prophets such as Ismail and Dhul-Kifl, honored as one of the best among Allah's servants. He is understood to have succeeded Ilyas in guiding the same community, continuing the mission of calling people back to the worship of Allah alone after Ilyas.

The Quran does not narrate a detailed account of Al-Yasa's life or the specific events of his prophethood, mentioning him only briefly among the list of those favored above the rest of creation for their righteousness and their patience in conveying Allah's message.

Even without a lengthy narrative attached to his name, Al-Yasa's inclusion among the Prophets honored in the Quran is itself a meaningful reminder - that steady, faithful continuation of a righteous mission, even without dramatic or widely-recorded events, is enough to be counted among those Allah has singled out for honor.
