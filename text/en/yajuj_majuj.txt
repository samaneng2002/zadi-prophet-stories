Ya'juj and Ma'juj (Gog and Magog) are described in the Quran as a large, destructive people who once spread corruption across the land, terrifying the settled communities near them until Dhul-Qarnayn built his great iron barrier to hold them back, sealing them away behind it for an age.

Allah tells us that this barrier will not last forever: as a sign of the approaching Hour, He will cause it to crumble, and Ya'juj and Ma'juj will pour out from every direction, exactly as foretold - "and they, from every elevation, descend." Their release is counted among the major signs preceding the Day of Judgment, alongside events like the coming of the Dajjal and the return of Isa (Jesus), peace be upon him.

The story of Ya'juj and Ma'juj is a reminder that every barrier humanity builds, however strong, is only ever temporary and stands solely by Allah's permission - and that the unfolding of these signs, however distant they may seem, is a fixed part of Allah's plan, meant to keep every generation mindful that the Hereafter is real and drawing nearer with each age that passes.
