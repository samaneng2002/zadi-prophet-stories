Yahya (John the Baptist, peace be upon him) was given to Zakariya and his wife in their old age as an answer to years of quiet supplication, and Allah named him even before his birth - a name no one had carried before him. From his earliest years, Allah granted him wisdom while he was still a child, along with tenderness, purity, and deep devotion to his parents.

The Quran describes him as dutiful and never arrogant or rebellious, a Prophet whose character combined firm commitment to the truth with genuine gentleness toward those around him. He was commanded to hold firmly to the Scripture given to him and to embrace it with earnest effort, and he grew into a Prophet known for calling people sincerely back to Allah, confirming the coming of Isa as a Prophet and Messenger who would follow him.

Yahya's life ended in martyrdom for standing firmly by the truth even when it endangered him, yet the Quran honors him with a striking greeting of peace upon him on the day he was born, the day he died, and the day he will be raised again - marking him as one of the Prophets held in the highest esteem despite the shortness of his earthly life.
