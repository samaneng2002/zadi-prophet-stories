Ayyub (Job, peace be upon him) was a Prophet blessed abundantly with wealth, family, and health, yet known above all for his gratitude to Allah. He was tested with the loss of his wealth, then his children, and finally his own health, enduring a prolonged illness that stripped him of nearly everything - yet through it all, he did not complain to people, and his patience became legendary.

Only his devoted wife remained faithfully at his side through the long years of hardship, tending to him despite their poverty. When his suffering became almost unbearable, Ayyub did not demand relief or protest his condition; he simply turned to Allah with humility, saying that hardship had touched him, and that Allah was the Most Merciful of the merciful.

Allah answered his call, commanding him to strike the ground with his foot, at which a spring of cool water burst forth for him to bathe in and drink, restoring his health completely. His wealth and family were restored to him as well, doubled in mercy for what he had endured. Ayyub's story stands as the timeless example of patience through affliction - not a patience of silence and resentment, but one of continued trust and gratitude toward Allah in every circumstance.
