Salih (peace be upon him) was sent to the people of Thamud, who lived in the aftermath of 'Aad and carved their homes skillfully out of mountains, prospering on the earth. Like the Prophets before him, Salih called his people to worship Allah alone, but they demanded a miracle to prove his truthfulness.

Allah brought forth a she-camel from solid rock as a sign for them - a clear miracle they could not deny. Salih warned his people to let her graze and drink freely, taking turns with their own water, and never to harm her, or a painful punishment would seize them. For a while some among them heeded the warning, until a group of them plotted together and killed the she-camel out of defiance and arrogance.

Salih told them they had only three days left before the punishment would come, and on the third day, exactly as warned, a tremendous blast and a mighty shaking overtook them, and they were left lifeless in their homes by morning. Only Salih and the believers were spared. His story stands as a reminder that a clear sign rejected out of pride brings its own swift consequence.
