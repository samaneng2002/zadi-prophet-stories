Ismail (Ishmael, peace be upon him) was the eldest son of Ibrahim, born to his wife Hajar. While Ismail was still an infant, Ibrahim, by Allah's command, left him and his mother Hajar in the barren valley of Makkah, where there was no water, no food, and no one to help them. Hajar asked Ibrahim only once whether Allah had commanded this, and upon hearing that He had, she said with full trust, "Then He will not let us be lost."

As Hajar ran desperately between the hills of Safa and Marwah searching for water for her thirsty child, Allah caused the well of Zamzam to spring forth beneath the infant Ismail's feet, and it has flowed ever since. Their presence in that valley eventually drew travelers and settlers, and Makkah grew around that very spring.

Years later, Ibrahim was commanded in a vision to sacrifice his son, and Ismail, upon hearing of it, urged his father to carry out what Allah had commanded, showing patience and complete submission as a young man. Allah spared him with a ram in his place. Ismail grew to help his father build the Kaaba, raising its foundations together stone by stone, and became a Prophet in his own right, known for keeping every promise he made.
