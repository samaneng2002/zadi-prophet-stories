Qarun (Korah) was a man from the people of Musa (Moses) who was given such vast wealth that even the keys to his treasuries were said to be too heavy for a group of strong men to carry. Rather than being grateful, he grew arrogant, telling his people that all of it was his own achievement, earned through his own knowledge and skill.

His own people warned him: seek the Hereafter through what Allah has given you, do not forget your share of this world, and do good as Allah has done good to you - and above all, do not seek corruption on earth. Qarun dismissed every word of advice, walking out among his people in his finest clothes and ornaments, and some who saw him actually envied his worldly glamour.

Allah then caused the earth to swallow him and his home whole, along with all his wealth, leaving him with nothing to save him and no one able to help him. Those very people who had envied him a day earlier then realized how close they themselves had come to the same trap, understanding that true success and provision come only from Allah - a lesson that remains as sharp today as it was then.
