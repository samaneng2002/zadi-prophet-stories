Zakariya (Zechariah, peace be upon him) was an elderly Prophet who had reached old age without a child, his wife likewise well past the years typically associated with childbearing. He served as a caretaker of the sacred sanctuary and was responsible for the upbringing of Maryam (Mary), and it was upon seeing Allah provide her with out-of-season fruit in her private chamber that Zakariya's own hope was renewed - if Allah could provide for her so easily, surely He could grant him a child despite every apparent obstacle.

He called upon Allah quietly, in the stillness of the night, asking not for himself alone but for an heir who would carry forward the mission of guiding people to Allah, careful even in this personal request to seek what would benefit his community, not merely his own household. Allah answered him with the glad tidings of a son, Yahya, and gave Zakariya a sign - that he would be unable to speak to people for three days despite being in perfect health, communicating only through gestures during that time.

Zakariya's story is remembered as one of quiet, persistent supplication late in life, and unwavering trust that Allah's power is not limited by ordinary circumstance - a hope that was rewarded with a son who would himself grow into one of the most honored Prophets.
