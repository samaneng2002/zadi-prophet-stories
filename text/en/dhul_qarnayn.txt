Dhul-Qarnayn was a righteous, powerful ruler whom Allah granted authority and the means to travel across the earth. He journeyed far to the west, where he found the sun appearing to set into a dark, muddy body of water, and among a people he was given the choice to punish or treat kindly - he chose justice, punishing wrongdoers while rewarding the righteous.

He then journeyed east, finding a people with almost no shelter from the sun, and later reached a place between two mountains where he found a people who barely understood any language, living in fear of a destructive people known as Ya'juj and Ma'juj (Gog and Magog). They begged him to build a barrier to protect them, offering him payment.

Dhul-Qarnayn refused payment, saying what Allah had already given him was better, and instead asked only for their labor. Using iron blocks fused together with molten copper, he built a towering barrier that Ya'juj and Ma'juj could neither climb nor tunnel through. He credited the achievement entirely to Allah's mercy, adding that when Allah's promise comes, He will level the barrier - a sign, throughout, of a ruler whose power never once overtook his humility.
