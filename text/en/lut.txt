Lut (Lot, peace be upon him) was a nephew of Ibrahim who was sent as a Prophet to the people of Sodom, a community that had fallen into an immorality no nation before them had openly practiced. Lut called them again and again to abandon their sin and return to decency, but his own people mocked him and threatened to expel him from the town for daring to criticize their ways.

When angels came to Ibrahim with the news that this people would be destroyed, they went on to visit Lut in the form of handsome young men, and Lut grew distressed, fearing for their safety among his own people, since he did not yet know they were angels. His people rushed toward his house in their old ways, but the angels revealed themselves and struck the intruders blind, then instructed Lut to leave with his family before dawn, without looking back.

Lut's own wife, who had secretly sided with the people rather than with him, was left behind and perished with them. At sunrise, the town was overturned upon itself, and a rain of stone struck the land - a fate reserved for those who persisted after every chance to change had already been given.
