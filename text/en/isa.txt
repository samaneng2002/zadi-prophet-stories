Isa (Jesus, peace be upon him) was born to Maryam (Mary) through a miracle of Allah's creative power alone, without a father - the same Allah who created Adam from clay without either a father or a mother. When Maryam, withdrawn from her people during the pains of childbirth, was accused upon her return of wrongdoing, she pointed silently to her newborn son, and Isa spoke from the cradle, declaring that he was a servant of Allah, given the Scripture and made a Prophet - defending his mother's honor before he had spoken a single word to anyone else.

Isa grew to be a Messenger to the Children of Israel, granted miracles by Allah's permission alone: healing the blind and the leper, giving life to the dead, and even shaping a bird from clay and breathing life into it - each one a sign, never a claim to divinity, since Isa himself repeatedly affirmed that he was only a servant and Messenger of Allah, calling his people to worship Allah alone, his Lord and theirs.

When his enemies plotted to kill him, Allah raised him up to Himself, and the one who was crucified was made to resemble him instead - Isa himself was not killed, nor was he truly crucified, but was saved and elevated. He is honored in the Quran as one of the mightiest of Messengers, and Islamic teaching holds that he will return before the Day of Judgment, affirming the truth he always taught: that worship belongs to Allah alone.
